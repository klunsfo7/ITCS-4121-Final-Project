Project Overview & Planned Insights

High-level story tying both datasets together

This section is for final presentation & report: briefly explain questions, data sources, and what each visualization reveals.

  • Mental health dataset: self-reported depression/anxiety plus CGPA, university, academic workload, and lifestyle factors.
  • Performance dataset: standardized math, reading, writing scores with demographic and support context like test prep and lunch type.
  • Dashboard design: one tab focusing on mental health patterns, one on score distributions, and one on synthesizing takeaways.
